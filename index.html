AI interpretation notice

These translations and cultural interpretations are AI-assisted working interpretations and may contain OCR, reading, translation, or context errors. Further testing and external references should be used to determine an accurate translation, especially for historical, regional, religious, or site-specific text.

Prototype note: these are hand-defined demonstration hotspots, not automatic OCR. The next version can let a visitor draw a box around any text region and send that crop through an OCR/translation pipeline.
